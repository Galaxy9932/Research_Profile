Proposal Summary • Fall 2027 Intake

Quantum Field Theory in Curved Spacetime and Effective Field Theory Approaches to Quantum Cosmology and Modified Gravity

Applicant: Soham Maity • Affiliation: Department of Physical Sciences, IISER Mohali

1. Motivation & Executive Summary

Understanding quantum fields in strong gravitational regimes remains one of the preeminent challenges in theoretical physics. While General Relativity and Quantum Field Theory each succeed brilliantly in their domains, unifying them requires rigorous semiclassical foundations. Quantum Field Theory in Curved Spacetime (QFTCS) serves as the indispensable bridge, generating profound insights into Hawking radiation, particle creation in expanding geometries, and vacuum polarization.

Cosmology provides the quintessential testing ground for QFTCS. In the inflationary epoch, extreme spacetime curvature implies that quantum vacuum fluctuations evolve directly into observable cosmological perturbations and cosmic microwave background anisotropies. Alongside General Relativity, extended theories—such as teleparallel and metric-affine gravity—introduce novel geometric degrees of freedom (torsion and non-metricity). An Effective Field Theory (EFT) formulation enables systematic, controlled inclusion of quantum corrections organized by energy scales.

2. Five Core Research Objectives

1. Quantum Field Dynamics in Curved Backgrounds: Analyze mode expansions, Bogoliubov transformations, and renormalized stress-energy tensors $\langle T_{\mu u} angle_{ m ren}$ on time-dependent cosmological metrics.
2. Cosmological Perturbations in Extended Gravity: Formulate gauge-invariant linear and non-linear perturbation equations in metric-affine and teleparallel gravity theories.
3. Effective Field Theory of Gravity: Construct low-energy EFT actions capturing higher-derivative quantum gravitational corrections classified by fundamental symmetries.
4. Early-Universe Physics, Decoherence & Inflation: Examine implications of scale invariance, environmental decoherence of primordial modes, and primordial black hole (PBH) generation.
5. Black Hole Perturbations & Stability: Compute quasinormal modes (QNMs) and stability criteria in extended gravity models as empirical probes of quantum gravity.

Manuscript in Preparation • REVTeX

Dynamical Systems in Teleparallel analog of Horndeski gravity

Soham Maity (IISER Mohali) and Jackson Levi Said (ISSA, University of Malta)

Department of Physical Sciences, IISER Mohali • Institute of Space Sciences and Astronomy, University of Malta

Teleparallel Horndeski GW170817 ($c_T = c$) Center Manifold Reduction $H_0$ and $S_8$ Tensions
View Repository
Abstract: We present a comprehensive and mathematically rigorous phase-space dynamical systems analysis of cosmological models within the framework of Teleparallel Horndeski gravity (BDLS theory). By coupling scalar field gradients non-minimally to the irreducible components of the torsion tensor ($v_\mu, a_\mu, t_{\lambda\mu u}$), the theory establishes the most general scalar-torsion action with second-order field equations. Crucially, these torsional derivative couplings naturally preserve luminal gravitational wave speed ($c_T = c$) without setting higher-order scalar-tensor interactions to zero, directly evading the constraints from GW170817. We perform an exhaustive classification of all hyperbolic and non-hyperbolic critical points across the phase space, apply Center Manifold Theory to fourth order, construct Lyapunov functions, and demonstrate heteroclinic trajectories resolving cosmological tensions.

Renormalizability of the Four-Fermi Weak Interaction at One-Loop Level in Four Dimensions

Soham Maity

Department of Physical Sciences, Indian Institute of Science Education and Research Mohali

Quantum Field Theory Fermi Interaction One-Loop Divergence
Abstract: A theoretical examination of Fermi's four-fermion interaction theory in $d=4$.
